## WeathaDock 1.1.0

**Much lighter.** WeathaDock has been rebuilt on Tauri, which uses the browser engine already in Windows instead of shipping its own. The installer is a fraction of its old size and the app uses far less memory.

**Customizable widget.** Pick from twelve ready-made styles or build your own: turn every element on or off, switch between vertical and horizontal, and choose size, alignment, text color and opacity, all with a live preview.

**Smoother to live with.** Lock the widget in place, snap it to screen edges, right-click it for quick actions, or keep it above other windows. The app window remembers its size, and the last forecast shows instantly at startup.

**Upgrading from 1.0.x:** uninstall the old version first (Settings, Apps, Installed apps), then run this installer once. Your locations carry over. From here on, updates install from inside the app.
