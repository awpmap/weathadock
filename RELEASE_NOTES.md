## WeathaDock 1.1.5

**A cleaner taskbar strip.** Everything on the taskbar now sits on one grid: each hour is an icon with the temperature above its time, lined up with the conditions and the high and low beside them.

**Make it yours.** With the weather on the taskbar, Settings now lets you turn the conditions, the high and low, and the rain timing on or off, and choose how many hours ahead to show: none, 2, 4 or 6. Alerts always show.

This update installs from inside the app: open Settings and choose Check for updates.

**Upgrading from 1.0.x:** uninstall the old version first (Settings, Apps, Installed apps), then run this installer once. Your locations carry over.
