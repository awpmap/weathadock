## WeathaDock 1.1.14

**Mouse battery in plain words.** The mouse battery no longer shows a percentage. It shows one of four levels instead: **Good**, **Mid**, **Low** and **Charge now**. Low turns amber, Charge now turns red, and each sends one notification. The level only changes when the charge has clearly moved, so it does not flip back and forth, and "Charging" still appears while the mouse is plugged in.

**No false alarms.** Low and Charge now only appear after three checks in a row agree, about a minute. A single wrong reading, or the first reading after the mouse wakes up, can never turn the level red or send a notification.

This update installs from inside the app: open Settings and choose Check for updates.

**Upgrading from 1.0.x:** uninstall the old version first (Settings, Apps, Installed apps), then run this installer once. Your locations carry over.
